< Adobe x JetBlue Case Study >

< Overview >

This case study details a strategic digital transformation proposal for JetBlue, where I acted as the Solutions Consultant to address customer experience fragmentation and fragmented data sources. This work included defining the ideal customer journey and recommending a strategy that utilizes the Adobe Experience Cloud and Creative Cloud to create unified customer profiles, enabling real-time personalization across every touchpoint from booking to in-flight experience.
