< Fintern >

< Overview >

Fintern is a web app designed to help students, interns, and young professionals take control of their finances during and after their internships. The platform allows users to track their payments, create personalized budgets, and receive smart suggestions on how to save more effectively. Fintern goes beyond basic budgeting by highlighting areas where users can cut unnecessary spending and recommending simple, beginner-friendly investment opportunities in stocks. Whether it’s managing day-to-day expenses or building a foundation for long-term financial growth, Fintern empowers users to make confident, informed financial decisions tailored to their goals and lifestyle.
