< Overview >

In the heart of downtown St. Louis, the National Blues Museum celebrates the story of blues music and its deep influence on American culture. The museum invites visitors to explore how the blues shaped genres like jazz, rock, and soul through interactive exhibits, personal stories, and musical recordings. Guests can even create their own blues song in the museum’s recording studio, connecting creativity with history. Building on this spirit of interaction, my project explores how augmented reality (AR) could enhance the visitor experience by bringing the stories, performances, and emotions of the blues to life. Through AR, visitors could see musicians perform in historical settings, visualize the evolution of instruments, or uncover hidden layers of storytelling woven throughout the museum. This approach aims to make the museum experience more immersive, personal, and memorable for every visitor.
