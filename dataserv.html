< Working at WashU Data Services >

< Overview >

WashU Libraries’ Data Services helps the university community explore, analyze, and visualize data through tools like 3D modeling, mapping, and extended reality. As a Student Data Visualization Assistant, I support these efforts by helping students and researchers bring their projects to life through creative and accessible uses of technology. But what do I actually do?

< What I Do Can Be Broken Down Into 3 Things >

3D Modeling and Photogrammetry
I work with 3D modeling and photogrammetry to digitally capture and preserve real-world objects. I photograph, process, and generate 3D models using Agisoft Metashape, refine and edit them in Blender, and contribute to the creation of interactive project pages for WashU’s Ezell Project.
Teach Users How to Use/Incorporate Mixed Realities Technology
I guide users in using mixed reality technology, including VR headsets like the Quest 2 and 3. I create guides, provide hands-on support with photography and podcasting equipment, and assist students in classes to explore these tools effectively.
Research New Tools and Technology
I stay on the lookout for new tools and technologies, from devices like the Artec Spider for more efficient 3D modeling to innovative data visualization software that Data Services can incorporate into projects.
